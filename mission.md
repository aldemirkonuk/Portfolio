# Mission — Aldemir Konuk

> *"I read the floor. I find the gap. I weigh the risk. I build there."*

## The one line
I build machine-learning systems that **see what others walk past** — that turn raw
footage and spatial data into the read, the gap, the decision. The portfolio is not a
résumé; it's proof of a way of seeing.

## Why me
Born on the Mediterranean coast. Professional basketball player. The court taught me
systems thinking, reading gaps under pressure, and acting before anyone else moves.
That instinct is the product: **find the mispriced gap, then build there.**

## What the work has to prove
- **It is ML, not a dashboard.** Every flagship piece learns from data — detection,
  tracking, value models, decision-grading — not hard-coded rules.
- **It is unique, not a tutorial reskin.** I fork the best open-source pipeline, then add
  a layer no one else has: reading the floor and pricing the risk.
- **It is mine.** The thesis (spatial intelligence, the gap, risk-vs-reward) runs from the
  basketball court through every line of code.

## Flagship — "Read the Floor"
A computer-vision system that watches basketball and renders the **value of the floor**
as a broadcast-grade overlay: who guards whom, where the gap is, what the best read is
worth, and what it risks. Built on a forked Roboflow/Skalski vision pipeline +
an original value-and-risk brain trained on open tracking data.
Lives in `Talent Between the Lines/`. Full spec: `Talent Between the Lines/READ_THE_FLOOR.md`.
First working prototype: `Talent Between the Lines/prototypes/matchup_engine.gif`.

## The bar for every decision
1. Does this learn from data? (If not, it's not the flagship.)
2. Does this do something the inspiration didn't? (If not, go deeper.)
3. Does a stranger land on it and think: *this person sees the game differently*?
